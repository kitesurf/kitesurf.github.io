<h2 id="withdrawal">Right of withdrawal for consumers</h2>
<p>Consumers have a right of withdrawal of <strong>14 days</strong> from the conclusion of the contract, without giving reasons. The withdrawal is to be addressed to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Kitesurf Mallorca SL</li>
  <li>Calle del Greco 19</li>
  <li>07400 Alcudia, Mallorca, Spain</li>
  <li>Email: team@kite-mallorca.com</li>
  <li>Phone: +34-696-264729</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Exception:</strong> For services (kite and wingfoil courses, private tuition, equipment rental) provided on a specific date or within a fixed period, the right of withdrawal lapses early if you expressly agree that we begin the service before the end of the withdrawal period and the service has been provided in full.</p>

<h2 id="cancellation">Cancellation &amp; rebooking by you</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Free cancellation or rebooking up to <strong>7 days</strong> before the start of the course — the deposit (20%) will be refunded in full.</li>
  <li>Later cancellation or no-show: the 20% deposit will be retained as a flat-rate expense allowance.</li>
  <li>Rebooking subject to availability: once free of charge up to 7 days before the appointment.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="weather">Cancellation by the kiteschool due to weather</h2>
<p>Kitesurfing depends on the wind. If the school cancels the session for safety or wind reasons, you can choose between free rebooking, a voucher, or a full refund of the deposit. If a started course is interrupted by the weather, only the time actually spent riding is charged (equipment rental: only time in the water).</p>

<h2 id="refunds">Refunds</h2>
<p>Refunds are issued via the same payment method used for the booking within <strong>10 business days</strong> of cancellation or withdrawal.</p>
