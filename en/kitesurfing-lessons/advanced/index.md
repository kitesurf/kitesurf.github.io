## Targeted Skill Modules

{% include carousel.html %}

* **Upwind Riding Mastery:** Stance adjustment, edge control, and effortless tacking back to your starting point.
* **Transitions & Turns:** Fluid sliding transitions, toeside riding, and carving jibes.
* **Jumping & Airtime:** Loading the edge, kite timing, pop, and soft, controlled landings.
* **Safety & Self-Rescue:** Deep-water pack down, light-wind relaunching, and self-landing techniques.

## What to Bring

Swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a beach towel, and **plenty of drinking water**! You are welcome to bring your own gear if you have it; otherwise, all current-season coaching equipment, wetsuits, harnesses, vests, and helmets are provided.
