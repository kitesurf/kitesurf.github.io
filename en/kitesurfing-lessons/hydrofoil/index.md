# Hydrofoil Kitesurfing Lessons in Mallorca

Hydrofoiling represents the ultimate sensation in kiteboarding: As the underwater hydrofoil wing creates hydrodynamic lift, the board rises completely out of the water. All surface chop disappears, and you cruise silently at high speed even in gentle 8–10 knot thermal breezes.

> **Course Summary:**  
> * **Rate:** **{{ p.hydrofoil_hourly.price }} {{ curr }}** / hour (includes complete foil equipment & coaching boat support).  
> * **Prerequisite:** Solid upwind riding and confident kite control on a twintip.  
> * **Fast Learning Curve:** With our short-mast method, most riders achieve stable cruising within 2 to 4 hours of instruction.

---

## Step-by-Step Foil Progression

{% include carousel.html %}

1. **Short School Mast:** We start with a shorter mast (45–60 cm) to eliminate the fear of height and ensure gentle touchdown control.
2. **Weight Distribution & Pitch:** Learn how subtle front-foot pressure controls ride height and prevents accidental breaching.
3. **Deep Water Boat Support:** Lessons take place in the open bay with our coaching boat, ensuring deep water and uninterrupted runs.

## What to Bring

Swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a beach towel, and **plenty of drinking water**! Complete hydrofoil boards, wings, wetsuits, harnesses, vests, and safety helmets are provided.
