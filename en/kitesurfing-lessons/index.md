# Learn Kitesurfing in Mallorca – Over 15 Years of Excellence

Located at Sa Marina beach in the Bay of Pollensa (between Alcúdia and Port de Pollença), KITE-MALLORCA is your premier destination for learning to kitesurf safely, efficiently, and with maximum enjoyment.

With small training groups (max. 2–4 students per coach), certified English-speaking instructors, and top-tier gear from Duotone and Eleveight, we guarantee optimal safety and fast progression.

## The 3-Step Progression

```
Step 1: Ground School & Control ➔ Step 2: Shallow Water Bodydrag ➔ Step 3: Water Start & Riding
```

1. **Beach Flight School:** Understand the wind window, master 4-line bar controls, and practice emergency safety release mechanisms.
2. **Bodydragging in Warm Water:** Feel the full pull of the kite as you steer through the water, practicing relaunch and board recovery.
3. **Board Start & Cruising:** Coordinate kite dive power with board edging to stand up and ride your first continuous runs.

---

## Why Choose Pollensa Bay for Your Lessons?

* **Vast Standing Area:** Sand seabed with waist-deep flat water enables stress-free learning without fatigue.
* **Consistent Afternoon Breeze:** The reliable "Embat" thermal wind blows almost daily from April to October (12–18 knots side-onshore).
* **Complete Gear Provided:** Current-season Duotone & Eleveight kites, boards, wetsuits, harnesses, vests, and helmets.
