## The Advantages of Private Coaching

* **100% Focused on You:** No waiting or sharing kite time with other students.
* **Instant Radio Feedback:** Real-time coaching tips directly through our communication helmet while riding.
* **Flexible Scheduling:** Sessions scheduled around your holiday itinerary and the best afternoon thermal wind window.

Swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, a beach towel, and **plenty of drinking water**! All technical gear, kites, boards, harnesses, wetsuits, and radio helmets are provided.
